Giving is an Act of

We believe that giving is more than just a financial transaction; it is a spiritual discipline that reflects our trust in God. When we give, we express our gratitude for His abundant blessings and partner with Him in the work of the gospel locally and globally.

"Each of you should give what you have decided in your heart to give, not reluctantly or under compulsion, for God loves a cheerful giver."
— 2 Corinthians 9:7
